Habsburg AI & Model Collapse

Why AI grading AI is a hazard: Inbreeding datasets on LLM outputs degrades subsequent models. If AI grades work generated by AI, feedback becomes a self-reinforcing, homogenous echo chamber.

The Recursive Degradation Cycle

🤖

GenAI Essay

Student prompts AI to write assignment using generic patterns.

➔
🖥️

AI Assessment

AI grader scores essay, matching standard patterns (inflated score).

➔
📉

Model Training

Output loop trains future models on mediocre, AI-on-AI data.

➔
💥

Model Collapse

Degraded diversity, loss of extreme capabilities, high hallucinations.

The Solution: Human-in-the-Loop

Human professors introduce nuance, identify AI style hallmarks, and break the recursive loop, preserving the high standards of academic assessment.

The Promise: Consistency + Compassion

Use AI to draft structure and identify typos (saving 70% grading time), and dedicate that saved time to direct student counseling and custom, high-impact edits.

The Overmarking Bias

LLMs are trained via RLHF to be highly agreeable (sycophancy). This causes them to systematically overmark student work, inflating grades for neatly formatted but shallow essays.
